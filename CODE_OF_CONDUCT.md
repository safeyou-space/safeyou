# Code of Conduct

Safe YOU exists to protect people from violence, abuse and harassment. Everyone who takes part in this project is expected to keep to that standard, in this repository and in the Safe YOU application.

## In this repository

This section applies to issues, pull requests, code review, discussions and every other place where people work together on this project.

Participants are expected to:

- treat each other with respect;
- comment on the work, not on the person;
- use welcoming and inclusive language;
- keep the safety and privacy of the people who use Safe YOU in mind in everything they contribute.

The following is not accepted:

- harassment, intimidation, threats or stalking of any kind;
- insults, hate speech and discriminatory remarks about sex, gender identity, sexual orientation, age, disability, ethnic origin, nationality, religion or any other personal characteristic;
- sexualized language or images, and unwelcome sexual attention;
- publishing another person's private information without their permission;
- sharing any information that could identify or endanger a person who uses Safe YOU.

## Reporting a problem

If you experience or see behavior that breaks this Code of Conduct, write to [info@safeyou.space](mailto:info@safeyou.space). Every report is reviewed, and the identity of the person who reports is kept confidential.

The project maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues, commits and other contributions that break this Code of Conduct, and may block a person from the project for a limited time or permanently.

## In the Safe YOU application

People who use Safe YOU, and the organizations and consultants who work in it, accept the rules below. Their full text is part of this repository:

- [Terms and Conditions](docs/policies/terms-and-conditions.md), accepted by every user at registration;
- [Privacy Policy](docs/policies/privacy-policy.md), accepted by every user at registration;
- [General Terms and Conditions on Content Development, Provision of Consulting and Online Moderation](docs/policies/content-consulting-and-moderation-terms.md), accepted by the organizations and consultants who publish content, give consultations and moderate the forums.

These rules protect users against abuse and harassment in the following ways:

- **Anonymity.** Other users see only the nickname a user chose at registration, not the name, surname or date of birth (Terms and Conditions, "How to start using the App?").
- **Prohibited content and conduct.** Abuse, threats, harassment, bullying, hate speech, discrimination, grooming, sexually explicit content and violent language are not allowed (Terms and Conditions, "Ground Rules"; General Terms and Conditions, clause 6.2).
- **Reporting.** Users can report content they find inappropriate, violent or misleading (Terms and Conditions, "Forums"; General Terms and Conditions, clause 8.2).
- **Blocking.** Users can block other users in the forums and stop seeing their comments (Terms and Conditions, "Block List").
- **Moderation.** The forums are moderated. Content that breaks the rules is removed, and an account that breaks them can be suspended or terminated (Terms and Conditions, "Ground Rules" and "Termination"; General Terms and Conditions, clause 6.4).
- **Complaints.** A complaint about published material, a comment, a consultant or another user is checked within three working days, and the person who complained is notified of the decision (General Terms and Conditions, clause 8.3).
- **Age groups.** Forum discussions and published materials are separated by age group: children aged 12 to 17, and adults (General Terms and Conditions, clauses 3.3 and 8.1).
- **Verified consultants.** Consultants are verified and shown with a special badge. Only representatives of organizations that work in child protection and have a child safeguarding policy may give consultations to children (Terms and Conditions, "Forums"; General Terms and Conditions, clauses 2.1 and 2.2).
