Personal Data shall be processed and stored for as long as required by the purpose they have been collected for. All data associated with an account in the App is stored for the validity of the account. If you delete your account in the App, access to it ends immediately, your profile is deactivated and hidden, and content you contributed, such as forum comments and alert records, is anonymized so that it can no longer be linked to you. If you request full erasure of your account and personal data, we complete it within 30 days of verifying your request. Deleted data also leaves our backups within 30 days, as backups run on a rolling cycle.

Personal Data collected for the purposes of our legitimate interests shall be retained for the validity of the account or until you send us a written request to stop processing your Personal Data.
