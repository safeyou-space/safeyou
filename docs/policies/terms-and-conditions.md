## What is Safe YOU?

Safe YOU is a multifunctional safety app for mobile device users and different stakeholders (NGOs, Governmental organizations, individual specialists), who provide support services to women and children to prevent and protect them in case violence.

The App permits you to send free alert SMS with your geolocation to up-to 7 prechosen contacts, including the Police.

## How to start using the App?

The first step is to choose the country and the preferred language available in the given country, then to proceed to the registration using mobile phone number.

To register you should fill in the necessary information: name, surname, birth date-(this information will not be available to other users, they will only see the nickname, created by you during registration).

To complete the registration, you should read and accept the terms and conditions and the privacy policy of Safe YOU application.

## What are the main functions of Safe YOU?

**Choosing Emergency Contacts:** In the Support section you can select up to three personal contacts from your phone’s contact list, up to three support contacts from the service provider organizations listed in the “Network” section of the App (Including collaborating governmental and non-governmental organizations), as well as enable the function to contact the Police.

**Help Function:** By pressing and holding the central button for three seconds the App will simultaneously send a free alert SMS with the user’s geo-location to up to 7 previously chosen contacts.

**Audio Recording:** Pressing and holding the central button for three seconds also enables the audio recording function for one minute. The audio record is kept in the recordings’ library and can be sent to service providers and the police or deleted at any time.

**Network:** In the Network section of the app you can find relevant information, such as contact details, hotlines, mapped locations, website and social media links of nearby women support and child protection NGOs, governmental organizations, and individual specialists verified by Safe YOU.

**Forums:**  In the “Forums” section of the App you can engage in anonymous peer-to-peer discussions in topical chat rooms and get consultation from women and children support organizations, as well as verified consultants such as lawyers, psychologists, doctors, and other counselors who are identifiable by a special badge.

The commenting functionalities include responding to other comments, editing or deleting your own comments, reacting to others’ comments by pressing the heart sign, as well as reporting the content that you may find as unappropriated, violent or misleading.

**Rating:** Users can rate the content in the Forums and leave an optional review text. Network members can also be rated, with a required review to be submitted- the rating and the review of the Network member can be later edited by the user.

**Block List:** To enable users to block any user generated content that they do not want to see. Users may block other users in the Forum sections and not see their comments anymore. All blocked users will be listed under this section. Any previously blocked users may be unblocked at any time.

**Security functions:** To enable safe usage of the App for users, who want to hide the App from others, Dual PIN and Camouflage functions are developed.

The Dual PIN allows you to create a real PIN to access the real app and a fake PIN that displays the photo gallery folder of the mobile device. The Camouflage setting changes the app icon and name on user’s mobile phone’s menu by displaying it as an image editing related app.

**User Profile:** In the Profile section of the App you can add/edit a profile picture, edit your personal data filled-in during the registration.

**Notifications:** The App provides in-app notifications for forum discussions, it also has a function of push notifications, which can be enabled or disabled by the users from the menu of the App.
