# SAFE YOU GENERAL TERMS AND CONDITIONS ON CONTENT DEVELOPMENT, PROVISION OF CONSULTING AND ONLINE MODERATION

© IMPACT INNOVATIONS INSTITUTE (Safe YOU)

## 1. General Terms

1.1. This document defines the rules and standards for content development, provision of consulting, and regulation of online moderation in the Safe YOU mobile application and the institutional dashboard (hereinafter jointly referred to as the Platform, individually as App and Dashboard respectively).

1.2. The rules defined in this document are applicable to physical persons and legal entities developing content and/or providing consulting and/or conducting moderation discussions in the Platform, within the scope of their obligations.

1.3. These rules are a written agreement between the physical persons and legal entities specified in Clause 1.2 and Impact Innovations Institute (the party co-managing the Platform). By accessing and using Safe YOU mobile app and Dashboar, physical persons and legal entities developing content and/or consulting and/or conducting moderation accept these terms and conditions in full. *The legal entities developing content and/or providing consulting and/or conducting moderation accept that the physical person providing consulting and/or creating content in the Platform on behalf of the given legal entity satisfies the conditions defined in Clause 2.1. of this document.*

1.4. Materials that do not meet the standards defined in the document may not be published on the Platform.

1.5. In cases where violations (partially or completely) of standards appear in already published materials, the materials are subject to modification (if possible) or removal in accordance with the provisions of Chapter 5.

1.6. In case of violation (partially or completely) of the rules and standards of providing online moderation and consulting in the Platform, the person who committed the violation is warned and in case of repetition of similar behavior, he/she is removed from the Platform.

## 2. Standards for Selection of Professionals as Content Developers and/or Consultants in the Platform

2.1. As a consultant and/or Contributor in the Forums section of the Platform may be accepted the persons, who:

- have higher education in relevant field;
- have at least 3-5 years of work experience in providing offline and/or online consulting with a narrow specialization/orientation;
- carry out their activities with a gender-sensitive and child-centered approach.

2.1.1. *Child-centered is the approach that is based on the existing international regulations and best practices in the field of protection of children's rights and best interests.*

2.1.2. *The approach is considered gender-sensitive when it ensures the inclusion of the gender equality component in the specific content. This may mean not only a clear prohibition of unequal legal relations between women and men, but also ensuring an intersectoral approach, including other grounds of differentiation (e.g. age, disability, ethnic origin, etc.) that may be related to sex and gender.*

2.2. Only representatives of non-governmental organizations and competent state bodies operating in the field of children's rights protection can provide consulting to child users, if the following conditions are met:

a) the representative meets the requirements defined in Clause 2.1 of the General Terms and Conditions, and

b) the public organization has a child safeguarding policy.

2.3. In case of equal to equal consultants in the Forums section of the Platform, the standard of having a higher education in the relevant field does not apply.

## 3. Technical Standards for Published Materials

3.1. The thematic materials (articles) published in the Platform must be within 600 words at most.

3.2. In order to make it easier to find the materials included in the Platform the published thematic materials should have a title that expresses the content of the article.

3.3. All the thematic materials published on the Platform should be separated according to age characteristics for children aged 12-17 and adults 18+, respectively.

## 4. Content Standards

4.1. Any material published in the Platform textual, audio, visual, must be based on a rights-based approach, excluding any manifestation of violence, as well as direct or indirect provisions and calls of hate speech. All materials intended for children must be guided by the principle of the best interest of the child.

4.1.1. *The approach is considered rights-based that is based on internationally established human rights, principles and norms.*

4.2. The published materials must be reliable and verified, based on field professional data, studies, research and other reliable sources. It is forbidden to publish any material about any person without the prior written consent of the latter and in case of children, without the prior written consent of their parents or guardians.

4.3. All published materials must be gender-sensitive, free from direct or indirect discriminatory formulations and expressions.

4.4. The publication of any material prohibited by law is prohibited. The party managing the Platform has the right after the publication of the prohibited material independently or on the basis of a report without prior notice to remove the prohibited material  and is obliged to inform the law enforcement authorities.

4.5. Any published material should be simple, accessible and competently written, excluding the use of unnecessary concepts, as well as slang words and expressions.

4.6. Any material published must take into account the age range of the audience.

4.6.1. The content of the thematic materials intended for children should meet all the characteristics of the mentioned group, have additional sensitivity and always be guided by the consideration of the best interest of the child and the principle of doing no harm.

4.6.2. Posting inappropriate material and/or content, namely harmful<sup>1</sup>, age-sensitive or illegal content is prohibited. In case of the publication of the prohibited material, the party managing the Platform is obliged to act in accordance with Clause 4.4 and in case of inappropriate<sup>2</sup> material and content, to remove them independently or on the basis of a report without prior warning.

<sup>1</sup> *Depending on the cultural context the material that is not harmful in one culture may be harmful at the time in another context. Considering this feature, the content can be published in the Platform only after ensuring that it is useful, harmless and effective content for the child user registered in the country.*

<sup>2</sup> *In contrast to prohibited materials, the demarcation of which is determined by the legislation of a given country, inappropriate materials may include materials that are not approved in terms of established norms and the resulting rules of conduct. Exceptions are those norms that are opposed to the constitution of the given country, ratified international treaties and the Universal Declaration of Human Rights.*

## 5. Modification of Content of Publications

5.1. Content changes of any thematic material included in the Platform shall be discussed and agreed upon with the author/authors of the material; without the consent of the author/authors of the material, it is forbidden to make any content changes in the materials of the Platform, except for the cases mentioned in Clauses 4.4 and 4.6.2.

5.2. In cases of violations (partial or complete) of the established standards in the published thematic materials, the materials are subject to modification (if possible) or removal.

5.3. If the author(s) of the thematic materials do not agree to make change in case of submitted valid proposals, the party managing the Platform may remove the thematic article from the Platform.

5.3.1. In the sense of these rules the proposal that is based on the existing international regulations and best practices in the field of protection of human dignity, fundamental rights and freedoms, the rights and best interests of the child, as well as professional researches and studies in the field is considered valid.

## 6. Ethical Rules for Online Moderation and Consulting

6.1. The persons and consultants included in the Forums section of the Platform are obliged:

- to respect human dignity, fundamental rights and freedoms, regardless of gender identity, sex, race, skin color, ethnic or social origin, genetic characteristics, language, religion, world view, political or other views, belonging to a national minority, property status, birth, disability , age, other personal or social circumstances;
- to exclude any manifestation of discrimination;
- to exclude any manifestation of violence;
- to exclude contacts with the child user outside the online platform, except for the case when a specialist representing a state body initiates a legal process ex officio.

6.1.1. *The language of the consultation is the official language of the given country and the moderator or consultant is under no obligation to provide partial or complete communication in another language.*

6.2. It is forbidden in the Forums:

- to insult, slander, show aggressive behavior towards each other;
- to engage in inappropriate behavior such as harassing or exploiting other people online, as well as cybercrime, trolling, content theft or any other activity that violates copyright and related rights;
- to conduct inappropriate communication: cybercrime, grooming
- to express and/or spread insulting, degrading, humiliating and/or hate speech towards other person/persons and/or groups,
- to discriminate against persons subjected to gender-based violence (including domestic and/or sexual violence, sexual exploitation and abuse), women, persons with disabilities, persons living with HIV, ethnic minorities or representatives of other groups and minorties.

6.3. The consultants, besides the mentioned ethical rules of online moderation, are obliged to maintain the confidentiality of personal and other types of protected information obtained and not to publish it in any way (verbally, in written form), not to transfer it to a third party/parties, not to directly or indirectly provide third party/parties with the opportunity to obtain that information, that is, to allow, not hinder or make it possible in any way, except for cases provided for in Clause 6.4.

In cases when during the online consultation data regarding a serious or particularly serious crime established by the legislation of the respective country, as well as data related to violence or threat of violence against a child become available to the consultant, the consultant is obliged to report about that to the law enforcement authorities *The consultant is obliged to inform the person to whom he/she provides consulting about the regulation mentioned in Clause 6.4.*

6.4. Any post and/or comment included in the Platform in violation of the ethical rules will be removed by notifying the person who made the post/comment and in case of repetition of similar behavior, the relevant person will be removed from the Platform, except for Clause 8.3.1, when the person is removed without warning.

## 7. Standards of Provision of Consulting

7.1. Counseling in the Platform is provided based on science-based, rights-based and gender-sensitive approaches and with the principle of doing no harm and the best interest of the child, observing the ethical rules mentioned in this document.

7.1.1. *The approach that is based on internationally established human rights, principles and norms is considered rights-based.*

7.2. The language of counseling should be simple, accessible and literate, excluding the use of unnecessary concepts, as well as slang words and expressions.

7.3. The consultants cannot provide consulting on any other issue outside of their professional direction, as well as the scope of the Forum topic. in case of any question outside of the professional field, as well as the scope of the Forum topic, the consultants are obliged to refer people, including relevant structures or other specialists involved in the Platform.

7.4. The reference should be carried out based on reliable science-based, rights-based and gender-sensitive approaches and with the best interest of the child, excluding the expression of one's (personal) beliefs, ideas and approaches in that process.

## 8. Ensuring Accountability and Control

8.1. Any person registered in the Platform (user) should have access to discussions and published materials in online Forums according to their age group.

8.2. In the event that there are content inaccuracies in the published materials and comments, the information provided is incomplete, as well as violations of the ethical rules or standards defined in this document, the users of the Platform and other registered legal entities and physical persons can send a report/complaint to the party managing the Platform about the published comments, clearly indicating the basis of the report.

8.3. Within a maximum of 3 (three) working days from the moment of receiving the complaint addressed to the user and/or other registered legal entity and physical person,

the party managing the Platform checks the content of the submitted complaint and processes it according to the following clauses (8.3.1-8.3.5).

8.3.1. In the event the violation of the ethical rules committed by the consultant is confirmed by the content of the addressed complaint, the consultant shall be removed from the Platform. In the event problems in the published material is confirmed by the content of the addressed complaint, the party managing the Platform shall undertake the rules for changing the materials included in the Platform (Clause 5).

8.3.2. In the event that the study of the addressed complaint confirms that there were inaccuracies in the consultant's post or comment, then the consultant edits the corresponding post/comment.

8.3.3. In the event that the content of the complaint confirms a violation of the ethical rules committed by one of the users, that person shall receive a notification about the violation of the ethical rules and shall be warned that in case of repeating such behavior, he/she will be removed from the Platform.

8.3.4. If the content of the addressed complaint confirms the situation mentioned in clauses 8.3.1-8.3.3, the person who submitted the complaint shall be notified about the relevant decision.

8.3.5. In the event that the content of the addressed complaint is not founded, the user shall be notified about the made decision through the Platform.

## 9. Accommodations For Persons with Disabilities

9.1. The Platform provides an opportunity to adapt content for people with disabilities.

9.2. Adaptations are made taking into account the different and special needs of people with disabilities: visual, auditory, mental, etc.

9.3. In order to ensure the inclusion and target addressability of the materials published in the Platform, the content materials can also be published in the form of pictures, recordings, short clips and other audio-visual methods.

10.2. The installment of any material, including visual images, in the Platform is possible only in case of compliance with the applicable legal requirements for the use and / or distribution of the said material. The party managing the Platform reserves the right to request relevant permission for the use of the result of intellectual activity used in the posted material and in case of the absence of such permission, to forbid or remove the relevant publication or its relevant part based on copyright protection considerations.
